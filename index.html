Features

Everything in one place.

The development map. The library. The calendar. The caregiver plan. All connected.

Development dashboard

Your child's progress across every domain, mapped to validated frameworks. Full view or Think / Relate / Move. See where they're strong. See where they need work. See the rate of movement over time.

Lesson library

Every quality enrichment activity from Khan Academy, Teachers Pay Teachers, PBS Kids, museum curricula, and library programs — in one place. Tagged by age and domain. Direct link to the provider. No content hosted. Always up to date.

Five Checks quality scoring

Every lesson is scored across five dimensions: Rigor, Inclusivity, Perspective, Empathy, Critical Thinking. The AI scores lessons automatically. Parents refine scores and add context — turning a 2.5 into a 5.

Developmental calendar

Plan by the day, month, quarter, or year. Recurring classes — swim lessons, gymnastics — sit alongside one-time events and open activity slots. The calendar becomes your child's developmental record as you log outcomes.

Caregiver mode — no login, no decisions

Share a 6-character code. Grandma opens the link. She sees today's plan — already made for her, based on your child's profile and regulation windows. What to do. What to say to start. What success looks like. What to do if it goes sideways. She executes. She doesn't decide. She feels capable. You see what happened.
