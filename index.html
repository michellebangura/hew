Features

Everything in one place.

The development map. The library. The calendar. The caregiver plan. All connected.

Development dashboard

Your child's progress across every domain, mapped to validated frameworks. Full view or Think / Relate / Move. See where they're strong, where they need work, and the rate of movement over time.

Lesson library

Every quality enrichment activity from Khan Academy, TPT, PBS Kids, museum curricula, and library programs — in one place. Tagged by age and domain. Direct link to the provider. No content hosted.

Five Checks quality scoring

Every lesson scored across five dimensions: Rigor, Inclusivity, Perspective, Empathy, Critical Thinking. AI scores on ingest. Parents add context to turn a 2.5 into a 5.

Developmental calendar

Plan by the day, month, quarter, or year. Recurring classes sit alongside one-time events. The calendar becomes your child's developmental record as you log outcomes.

Caregiver mode — no login, no decisions

Share a 6-character code. Grandma opens the link. She sees today's plan — already made for her. What to do. What to say to start. What success looks like. She executes. She doesn't decide. She feels capable. You see what happened.
